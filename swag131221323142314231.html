Official Governance Preamble

The Student Welfare Associative Group (S.W.A.G.) operates as the official, democratic liaison body established to bridge communication, operational affairs, and welfare policy between the institutional school staff and the broader student body. Through structured governance and formal dialogue, S.W.A.G. advocates for student rights, transparent welfare initiatives, and harmonious institutional development.
